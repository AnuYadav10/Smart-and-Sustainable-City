About Our Project

A city faces many everyday problems such as damaged roads, water leakage, broken street lights and cleanliness issues. Sometimes citizens do not know where or how to report these problems.

The Smart & Sustainable City portal provides a simple platform where citizens can report common city problems and get useful information about city services.

The main aim of this project is to encourage citizens to participate in improving their city and to support the idea of clean, safe and sustainable communities.

Our Objectives

  • Provide an easy way to report city problems.
  • Spread awareness about sustainable living.
  • Encourage citizens to participate in city improvement.
  • Provide useful information about city services.
  • Support the development of sustainable communities.

SDG Connection

SDG 11 – Sustainable Cities and Communities

This project is mainly connected with SDG 11 because it focuses on making cities more inclusive, safe, resilient and sustainable. The portal encourages citizens to take part in solving local problems and improving their surroundings.
